📋 10 Intake Sections

  • Basic Info (height, weight, waist, hip, age, dietary preference)
  • Goals (physical, nutrition, wellbeing, motivation)
  • Habits & Lifestyle (cooking, exercise, injuries, steps, alcohol)
  • Eating Patterns (typical day, weekends, snacking, eating out)
  • Medical History (family, medications, conditions)
  • Stress & Sleep (stress level, HRV, sleep quality, coping)
  • Gut Health (symptom scores, food sensitivities)
  • Bloodwork (all markers, out-of-range flagging)
  • GHQ-28 (4 subscales, depression threshold for referral)
  • Emotional Health (emotional eating, body image, self-talk)

⚙ Features

  • Continuous scroll form (not multi-page)
  • Conditional branching (show/hide questions)
  • Save and resume (currentSection tracking)
  • Voice input for narrative sections (Groq Whisper)
  • Progress bar with section reframing
  • IKEA effect: client picks own food record
  • Coach view: see all client intakes + status
  • Auto-compute: BMI, WHR, GHQ-28 subscales
  • Red flag detection (Groq Llama 3.3 70B)

✍ Daily Check-in UX

  • "How was your day?" (not "Daily Check-in Form")
  • Habit cards: yes / partly / not today
  • Metric inputs: number, select
  • Reflection: rotating prompts (7 options by day)
  • Under 2 minutes for MINIMAL mode
  • Pre-populates if already checked in (edit mode)
  • Gentle streaks: "5 of 7 days"
  • Max 3 loss signals

Behavioral design (non-negotiable)
💛 No shame, no blame. "Not today" not "Missed." Stone/amber palette only. No red/green adherence coding.
⛔ Anti-patterns. No badges, leaderboards, points, or gamification of any kind. Journal, not dashboard.

📊 Aggregation

  • Pull 7 days of DailyCheckIn for client
  • Compute habit adherence (gentle framing)
  • Summarize metric trends (water, steps, sleep)
  • Extract reflection themes
  • Structured SQL queries, not vector search

📝 Synthesis + Citations

  • AI generates narrative: "Protein in 18 of 21 meals (up from 14 last week)"
  • Every claim backed by citation to specific check-in data
  • Celebrate behavioral wins first
  • 1-2 small activators for next week
  • Frame as "Reassess" phase of PAR cycle

🔔 Proactive Nudges

  • Rule-based triggers (not AI-generated spam)
  • Client misses 3 days: coach gets flagged, not client bombarded
  • Coach sends nudge templates: "Hey, haven't heard from you. Everything okay?"
  • Behavioral readiness: resource library links timed by coaching readiness
🛡 Coach controls nudges. System flags, coach decides. No automated client messages.
